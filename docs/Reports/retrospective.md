# Sprint 1 Retrospective — GradeTracker

**Team 1:** Anthony Alperov and Netonna Udezeh  
**Sprint:** September 15–October 12, 2026  
**Retrospective meeting:** October 13, 2026

## Sprint Overview

For Sprint 1, we focused on getting the basic grade management features working. Netonna worked on retrieving student grades, while Anthony worked on entering and validating grades for instructors. We finished both features, merged our work into the main branch, and completed all 8 story points we committed to.

## What Went Well

We divided the work clearly, so each of us knew what we were responsible for. Using GitHub Issues and pull requests helped us organize our changes and combine our work. FastAPI, SQLite, and PyTest also made it easier to build, demonstrate, and test the backend features.

## What We Could Improve

Our biggest issue was keeping up with documentation during the sprint. We forgot to save Kanban board screenshots at the beginning and midpoint, so we only have the final board and GitHub activity as evidence. We also want to check our progress more consistently instead of leaving documentation until the end.

## Action Items for Sprint 2

1. **Save Kanban evidence:** Both teammates will capture dated board screenshots at the start, midpoint, and end of the sprint.
2. **Schedule a midpoint check-in:** Both teammates will review progress, blockers, and issue status halfway through the sprint.
3. **Check work before marking it Done:** The person implementing a feature will run its tests, and the other teammate will review the pull request before the issue is moved to Done.

## Closing Thoughts

Sprint 1 gave us a working backend foundation for GradeTracker and more practice collaborating through GitHub. For Sprint 2, our goal is to keep that momentum while improving communication and documenting our work as we go.
